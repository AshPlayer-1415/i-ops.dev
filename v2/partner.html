/   partner

Build this with us.

I‑Ops is early and it is being built in the open where it can be. If you have a workflow worth governing, an opinion about how this should work, or time you want to put into it, there is a way in.

/01   design partner

You have work you do not trust an agent with yet.

A real workflow, real constraints, and a reason nobody has automated it. We shape the runtime around it and you see everything we find, including the parts that do not work. No cost, and no obligation at the end of it.

/02   build with us

You want to work on the runtime itself.

Engineers, and people who think carefully about verification, permissions and failure. Some of this is open source and some is not. Tell us which part interests you and what you would want to own.

/03   integrate

You have an agent and no way to prove it behaved.

The guarantees are computed outside the worker, so they can apply to yours. Integration depth decides how much can be held, and the runtime says which guarantees it can hold rather than claiming all of them.

/04   advise

You work somewhere this has to be defensible.

Regulated industries, audit, legal, clinical. The people who ask what happens when it is wrong are the ones worth listening to early, and mostly they are not being asked.

/05   use the open pieces

You want to check the claims before talking to anyone.

Parts of the assurance layer are published and installable. Read them, run them against your own work, and tell us where they are wrong. That is the most useful thing anyone has done for us so far.

/06   something else

You have a reason we have not thought of.

Say it in the last box. Most of what has changed the direction of this product arrived from someone who was not in any of the categories above.

/   reach out

Tell us which one you are.

Everything here goes to one person and gets a reply. If a form is the wrong shape for what you want to say, hello@i‑ops.dev works just as well.
