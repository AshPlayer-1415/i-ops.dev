Privacy Policy

I-Ops is an AI worker operating layer for business teams. We are building a product designed to help teams run governed AI workers without becoming AI engineers, while the organization retains control over models, permissions, data, and execution.

Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how I-Ops handles information collected through our website, private access process, and related communications.

2. Website Information We May Collect

When you request access or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Work email
  • Company name
  • Role, location, and details you choose to submit through the private access form
  • Basic analytics or technical information, such as browser, device, and usage data, if analytics are enabled
  • If you choose to sign in or contact support from within the app, basic account information such as your name and email, plus any details you include in a support message

3. Product and File Handling

I-Ops is designed for company-controlled AI worker execution. Data and context processed through the product are intended to remain under customer policy on approved infrastructure, private systems, or supported external providers as your organization configures. Your company chooses which models and workers run where.

We do not use the marketing website to routinely collect the contents of customer business files or worker execution data.

4. How We Use Information

We may use submitted information to:

  • Respond to private access requests
  • Communicate about product access, updates, and availability
  • Improve the product and website
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse or abuse

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with essential service providers, such as website hosting, form processing, email, or analytics providers, only as needed to operate the website and communicate with users.

6. Retention

We retain inquiry and contact information only as long as needed for business, product access, support, and operational purposes.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information. No internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can contact us to request updates or deletion of submitted contact information where applicable.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will post the revised version on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@i-ops.dev. You can also use the private access request form.
