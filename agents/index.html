I-Ops

I-Ops makes AI workers finish real tasks correctly: it works out what context a task needs, acquires what is missing under the company's own permissions, governs consequential actions, and then independently verifies the outcome.

The principle underneath it: the worker that did the work does not get to declare it complete. The checks are computed by code, so swapping the model does not change the guarantee.

What we do not claim

  • Package download counts are mostly crawlers and mirrors. We do not report them as users.
  • The desktop app is an MVP in private testing. Some layers are designed and not built, and the documentation says which.
  • Nothing here governs an agent on a machine we cannot see, and we do not claim a sandbox.
